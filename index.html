About

I am a PhD candidate in Integrated Circuit and Systems Design at Fudan University, based in the State Key Laboratory of Integrated Chips and Systems and advised by Prof. Keren Zhu. I am currently a visiting PhD student at the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh, hosted by Dr. Jianyi Cheng.

My research is on electronic design automation (EDA), and specifically on making high-level synthesis (HLS) smarter. HLS compiles C/C++ into RTL, but its quality still lags behind hand-written designs: the design space is combinatorially huge, optimization stages are siloed, and the compiler is blind to the downstream tools and the target architecture. I work on learnable cost models and search strategies that close this gap — combining Bayesian optimization, graph neural networks, and more recently large language models that read the source code the way a hardware engineer would.

My work is developed in close collaboration with industry, including Shanghai Fudan Microelectronics and UniVista Industrial Software, which gives it real production designs and toolchains to be validated against.

Research Interests

Design Space Exploration for HLS

Learning-based search over pragmas and directives — Bayesian optimization and multi-model cost estimation to find high-quality latency/area trade-offs without exhaustive synthesis.

LLMs for Hardware Design

Using large language models to read HLS source code semantically, guide the search, and automatically repair or rewrite designs — bringing code-level understanding into a traditionally numeric optimization loop.

Architecture-Aware EDA

Cross-stage co-optimization from HLS through logic synthesis to place-and-route, making early decisions aware of downstream tools and the target architecture (FPGA, and beyond).
